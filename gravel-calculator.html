Gravel Calculator – Calculate Cubic Yards of Gravel & Crushed Stone

Installing gravel driveways, patio sub-base and garden rock paths requires accurate volume estimation. Under-ordering bulk gravel leads to incomplete projects, while over-ordering wastes money and delivery fees. This free tool calculates how many cubic yards of gravel you need based on surface area and paving depth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How deep should a gravel driveway be?

A: Regular vehicle driveways need 6–8 inches compacted gravel; light foot traffic paths only require 3–4 inches.

Q: Should I add extra volume for compaction?

A: Gravel compacts roughly 10–15%. Use the waste percentage setting to account for compaction and spreading losses.
