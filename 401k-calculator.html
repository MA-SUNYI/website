401(k) Calculator

The employer match is the closest thing to free money in personal finance: contribute enough to capture the full match and you instantly earn a 50% or 100% return on those dollars before the market even opens. This calculator projects your balance to retirement age, separating what came from **your** contributions, the **employer match**, and pure **investment growth**. Enter your details to see how the account compounds.
