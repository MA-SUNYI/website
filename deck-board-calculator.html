Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why do wood decks require larger gaps between boards than composite?

A: Natural lumber absorbs moisture and expands during humid weather; wider gaps prevent buckling. Composite material has minimal seasonal movement.

Q: Does this calculator include support joists, posts and stair materials?

A: Calculations only cover walking surface deck boards. Separate calculations are needed for framing, railing and stair components.

Q: What waste percentage should I use for diagonal herringbone deck patterns?

A: Diagonal layouts create many short cutoffs; select minimum 15–20% waste factor for angled decking patterns.

Q: Are there daily limits calculating multiple separate deck sections for multi-tier outdoor builds?

A: Zero usage caps, homeowners can run unlimited deck material calculations free for all outdoor construction projects.
