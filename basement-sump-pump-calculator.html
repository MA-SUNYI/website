Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does vertical lift reduce pump performance?

A: Each foot of vertical discharge reduces pump flow rate, so deeper basements need higher base GPM ratings.

Q: What size pump do most average basements use?

A: Standard 1/3 HP pumps deliver ~60 GPM for small basements; 1/2 HP handles larger heavy rainfall homes.
