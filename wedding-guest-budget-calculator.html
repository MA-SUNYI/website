Wedding Guest Budget Calculator – Estimate Wedding Total Cost By Guest Count

Wedding catering, food and beverage expenses scale directly with your guest list size. Many couples underestimate total wedding spending because they fail to multiply per‑guest costs by their full expected guest count. This wedding guest budget calculator computes projected main event costs, and adds a contingency buffer for unexpected wedding‑day expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What costs does this calculator cover?

A: It estimates venue, food and beverage core reception costs. It does not include dress, photography, flowers, rings and honeymoon expenses.

Q: Why add a contingency buffer for weddings?

A: Last‑minute add‑ons, overtime charges and small vendor adjustments are very common for wedding events, 10‑15% buffer is industry‑standard advice.
