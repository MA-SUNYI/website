Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much soil depth do vegetables need?

A: Leafy greens need 6–8 inches; root vegetables such as carrots require 10–12 inches of loose soil.

Q: Why add a waste percentage?

A: Soil settles after watering, and uneven ground creates gaps. 5%–10% extra material covers settling and spreading losses.
