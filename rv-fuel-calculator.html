Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why do Class A motorhomes get such low fuel mileage?

A: Heavy weight, large frontal area and big engines significantly increase fuel consumption compared to smaller camper vans.

Q: Should I add extra fuel consumption for mountain driving, headwinds or heavy cargo?

A: For steep mountain routes or fully loaded RVs, add 10–15% extra fuel cost manually to account for reduced real-world MPG.

Q: Can I use this calculator for travel trailers towed by pickup trucks?

A: You may manually select a lower MPG value matching your tow vehicle’s real-world mileage while pulling a trailer for reasonable estimates.

Q: Are there daily limits on calculating fuel budgets for multiple vacation route options?

A: Zero usage caps, RV travelers can run unlimited fuel cost comparisons free for all planned road trips year-round.
