Concrete Stair Calculator – Design Safe Porch & Patio Concrete Steps

Building outdoor concrete stairs requires balanced riser height and tread depth to follow building safety guidelines. Uneven step heights create trip hazards. This calculator computes ideal number of steps, riser height and horizontal tread length based on your total vertical elevation change for porch, patio and walkway concrete stair projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the safe range for outdoor stair risers?

A: Standard guidelines: minimum 4 inches, maximum 7.75 inches riser height.

Q: How deep should concrete stair treads be?

A: Outdoor tread depth commonly 10–14 inches for comfortable walking.
