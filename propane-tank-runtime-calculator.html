Propane Tank Runtime Calculator – Estimate How Long Your Propane Supply Will Last

Grill owners, campers and homeowners frequently run out of propane unexpectedly because they cannot easily calculate tank runtime. Every propane appliance has a unique BTU hourly burn rate, and different tank sizes hold varying gallons of fuel. The standard conversion of 91,500 BTU per gallon of propane makes manual math complicated for casual users. This free propane calculator accepts usable tank gallons, appliance BTU rating and average daily operating hours, then outputs total continuous burn hours and estimated number of days until the tank is empty for grills, patio heaters, camp stoves and residential furnaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many BTU per gallon of liquid propane?

A: Standard accepted value: 91,500 BTU per US gallon of propane fuel.

Q: Why is a 20lb tank only filled to 4.7 gallons instead of full weight?

A>Propane tanks require expansion space; suppliers only fill to 80% of rated volume for safety regulations.

Q: Does low flame / partial power reduce BTU consumption?

A>This calculator uses maximum BTU rating; lower heat settings extend runtime significantly.
