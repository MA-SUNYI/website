Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What benefit does staggered offset planting provide?

A>Staggered rows allow roughly 10–15% more plants in the same bed area with equal plant spacing clearance.

Q: Why should I buy extra seeds beyond total plant count?

A>Not every seed germinates; pests, drought and poor soil cause seedling failure, so a seed buffer prevents empty spots.

Q: Does this calculator work for square foot gardening layouts?
