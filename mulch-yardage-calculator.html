Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What mulch depth stops weeds effectively?

A: A 3-inch uniform mulch layer blocks most weed seeds from germinating in garden soil.

Q: How much waste should I add for mulch delivery?

A: Flat rectangular beds need 5–8% waste; sloped, irregular landscaping areas require 10–12% extra mulch.
