Blog platforms impose their own limits — post excerpts, meta descriptions, SEO titles and social previews each have a different cap — and a piece that reads well can still get truncated at the wrong moment.

The practical approach is to draft each field separately and check it against its own constraint. An SEO title is best under 60 characters, a meta description under 155, a social preview under 100 for some platforms and 280 for others. One article therefore produces four separate character checks, not one.

Beyond field limits, there is a quality dimension most tools ignore. A meta description that simply repeats the title wastes the opportunity to differentiate in search results. The most effective descriptions state a concrete benefit or answer a question, which is a writing decision rather than a counting one — but the count is what forces the discipline.

Frequently Asked Questions

What character limits apply to a blog post?

SEO title about 60 characters, meta description 155 to 160, social previews from 100 to 280 depending on platform. Post body limits are usually generous.

Should I count the whole post or individual fields?

Each field has its own limit, so check them separately. A total character count is rarely the constraint that matters.

Does the counter include HTML tags?

Plain text pasted into the counter excludes tags. Some CMS fields count the HTML source, which can consume a large share of the allowance.

How long should a blog post be?

Long enough to answer the query completely. Word count correlates with ranking only weakly; comprehensiveness is what matters.
