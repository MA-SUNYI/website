Dividend Calculator

Dividend investing has a quiet superpower: when you **reinvest** the payouts (a DRIP), you buy more shares, those shares pay more dividends, and the income snowballs. The key metric long-term holders watch is **yield on cost** — your annual dividend divided by your original investment, which rises every year the dividend grows. Enter your numbers to project the future value with or without reinvestment.
