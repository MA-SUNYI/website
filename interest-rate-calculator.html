Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the difference between simple and compound interest?

A: Simple interest only calculates earnings on the original principal; compound interest adds earned interest back to the balance to generate extra returns each year.

Q: Can I use this calculator to estimate multi-year mortgage total interest?
