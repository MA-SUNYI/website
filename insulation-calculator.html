Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much waste should I add for attic insulation?

A: Standard simple attic:8–10% waste. Attics full of plumbing and vent openings use 12–15% extra insulation.

Q: What insulation type is better for old attics?

A: Blown loose-fill insulation fills gaps better than fiberglass rolls for uneven older roof framing.
