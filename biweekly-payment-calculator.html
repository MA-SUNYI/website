Biweekly Payment Calculator

Paying **half your monthly payment every two weeks** sounds the same as monthly — but there are 52 weeks in a year, so you make **26 half-payments, equal to 13 full payments** instead of 12. That one extra payment a year attacks the principal early and can cut a 30-year loan by several years. Enter your loan amount, rate and term to see the interest saved and the time shaved off.
