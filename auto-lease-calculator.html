Auto Lease Calculator

A lease payment is just two parts added together: the **depreciation** you eat while driving the car, plus the **finance charge** on the money the lessor has tied up. Enter the vehicle price, your down payment, the residual value (what the car is worth at lease-end), the money factor and the term to see the real monthly number — including sales tax — and the total cost of the lease.

Tool Features

  • Monthly depreciation from price minus residual value
  • Monthly finance charge from the money factor

  • Total lease cost and cost per mile driven
