How To Use This Recipe Scaler

This portion calculator uses three simple inputs to instantly recalculate ingredient measurements, usable on both desktop computers and mobile phones while cooking in the kitchen.

Step 1: Enter Original Recipe Servings

Type the serving count printed on your original recipe. For example, if a cookie recipe makes 24 cookies, input 24 as the original serving value.

Step 2: Input Your Desired Target Servings

Fill in how many servings you actually want to make, whether you need a smaller batch or a large party-sized quantity.

Step 3: Add Base Ingredient Amount & Calculate

Type the original ingredient measurement (cups, grams, tbsp) and click Scale Ingredient to view your adjusted scaled amount ready for cooking.
