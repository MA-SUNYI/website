How the grade target works

Enter your current grade, its weight, and the grade you want for the course. The calculator solves for the score you still need on the remaining weight to hit the target.

Why it matters

Students often assume one bad mark ruins the term. Seeing the required final score shows whether the goal is still reachable — or mathematically impossible — so you can change strategy early.

Common mistake

Forgetting weights. A 20% final moves your average far less than a 50% one; the calculator uses the real weight, not a simple average.

FAQ

What if it says "impossible"? It means even a perfect remaining score cannot reach the target; you would need extra credit or to redo a weighted item.

Can I plan for multiple remaining items? Combine their weights as one remaining block; the result is the average you need across them.
