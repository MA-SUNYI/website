Common Problems & Solutions

  • Sitemap opens in browser but cannot be crawled: Vercel blocks Googlebot
  • 403 Access Denied: Disable Deployment Protection in Vercel Settings
  • Missing robots.txt: Use our generator to create file and upload
  • Discovered not crawled: Resubmit sitemap in Google Search Console
  • Vercel free tier: No bot protection switch, just turn off all access limits

Operation Guide
