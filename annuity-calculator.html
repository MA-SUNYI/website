Annuity Calculator

An annuity is just a stream of equal payments — and the same formula works whether you are building one (saving a lump sum plus monthly deposits) or spending one (turning a lump sum into a steady monthly check). This calculator does both. Use the accumulation side to project what your savings become, and the payout side to see how big a lump sum you need to generate a target monthly income for a set number of years.

Tool Features

  • Accumulation: future value of a lump sum plus monthly contributions (monthly compounding)
  • Payout: lump sum needed to fund a fixed monthly income for N years
  • Splits each result into principal, interest earned and total paid out
  • Ordinary-annuity math (payments at period end)

Common Usage

  • Plan the income side with the Retirement Calculator
