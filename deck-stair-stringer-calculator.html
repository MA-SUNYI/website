Deck Stair Stringer Calculator – Count Stringers & Find Safe Spacing

Building outdoor deck stairs requires correctly spaced stair stringers to meet residential building codes. Too few stringers create wobbly treads that fail inspections. This calculator uses your total stair width and standard maximum on-center spacing to output the exact number of pressure-treated stringers you need to purchase for porch and patio stairs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the legal maximum spacing for deck stair stringers?

A: Nearly all US IRC codes require stringers spaced no more than 16 inches on center for solid wood treads.

Q: Do wider stairs need extra middle stringers?

A: Yes, any stair section wider than 16 inches between supports must add intermediate stringers to prevent tread sagging.
