Length conversion crosses two systems whose units do not relate cleanly. The inch was fixed at exactly 25.4 millimetres in 1959, which makes imperial-to-metric arithmetic exact, but many people still work with rounded factors and accumulate error.

Useful exact relationships: 1 inch = 25.4 mm, 1 foot = 30.48 cm, 1 yard = 0.9144 m, 1 mile = 1.609344 km, 1 nautical mile = 1.852 km. The nautical mile is defined by one minute of latitude rather than by any land measurement, which is why it does not fit the pattern.

Precision matters most at small scales. Rounding 25.4 mm to 25 introduces a 1.6% error, which is enough to ruin a machined fit. In construction and engineering, convert using the exact factors and round only at the final step — mid-calculation rounding is how dimensions drift out of tolerance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many millimetres are in an inch?

Exactly 25.4. The relationship has been fixed by international agreement since 1959, so conversions in this direction are exact.

How many kilometres is a mile?

1.609344. A common approximation of 1.6 is accurate to about 0.6%, adequate for driving estimates but not for surveying.

What is a nautical mile?

1,852 metres, defined as one minute of latitude. It is used in navigation because it relates directly to coordinates.

Why do my conversions disagree slightly between tools?

Because some tools use rounded factors such as 0.3937 inches per centimetre rather than the exact value. Use exact factors where precision matters.
