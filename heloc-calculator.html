Home Mortgage Affordability

HELOC Calculator

A home equity line of credit works in two phases. During the **draw period** you usually pay interest only; during **repayment** the balance amortizes like a mortgage. Enter your home value, your first-mortgage balance and the rates to see the largest line you qualify for (capped by an 85% combined loan-to-value limit), your interest-only draw payment and the monthly repayment payment.

Tool Features

  • Maximum credit line from an 85% combined loan-to-value cap
  • Available equity after your first mortgage
  • Interest-only monthly payment during the draw period
  • Amortized monthly payment during repayment
  • Estimated total interest across both phases
