How To Use This Paint Coverage Calculator

This painting estimator uses four simple inputs to generate precise paint gallon totals, usable on mobile phones while measuring room dimensions on-site.

Step 1: Input Wall Width & Height Measurements

Enter total combined width of all four room walls and standard wall height in feet to calculate raw total wall surface square footage.

Step 2: Subtract Window & Door Cutout Area

Add together all square footage covered by windows, closet doors and entry doors that do not require paint coverage.

Step 3: Select Number Of Paint Coats & Compute

Choose 1 coat for primer only or 2 coats for full color finishing, then click Calculate Paint Gallons to view final paint volume including wastage buffer.
