Rent vs Buy Calculator

Is it cheaper to rent or buy? Compare the two over the years you plan to stay. The calculator tracks rent paid and rent growth against the cost of owning — down payment, loan payments and upkeep — versus the equity and appreciation you build.

  • Model rent growth and home appreciation

  • Test if high rent beats a big mortgage
