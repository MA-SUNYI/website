Student Loan Calculator

A student loan statement shows the balance and the rate, but rarely the number that decides your budget: the monthly payment, and what the loan really costs by the time it's gone. If you have several loans, add up the balances and use your weighted average rate. Enter the total below to see your payment, total interest and total cost — plus how a longer or shorter term changes everything.
