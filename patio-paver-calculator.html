Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many extra pavers should I buy?

A: Always add 8–12% extra pavers. Curved edges and irregular cuts create waste during installation.

Q: Do I need sand under patio pavers?

A: Yes, a 1-inch layer of bedding sand keeps pavers level and prevents shifting over time.
