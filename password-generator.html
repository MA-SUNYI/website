FAQ

Is this password generator safe?
Yes. All passwords are generated locally in your browser, no data is uploaded or stored on any server.

What is the best password length?
We recommend 16 characters or longer with mixed letters, numbers and symbols for strong security.

Can I create passwords without symbols?
Absolutely. You can uncheck the symbol option to generate letter and number only passwords.
