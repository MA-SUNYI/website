Roof Rafter Calculator – Calculate Rafter Length & Framing Lumber

Calculating roof rafter length manually requires complex right triangle math based on roof span and pitch. Many DIY builders mismeasure rafters and waste expensive framing lumber. This calculator computes the diagonal rafter run and estimates total rafter quantity for gable and shed roof construction for garages, sheds and small residential outbuildings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What roof pitch works best for shingles?

A: Asphalt shingles require minimum 4/12 roof pitch for proper rain runoff drainage.

Q: 16 vs 24 inch rafter spacing difference?

A: 16in spacing creates stiffer roof sheathing; 24in reduces lumber cost for light load sheds.
