Loan Comparison Calculator

Two loans can look similar on the sticker but land worlds apart on the total bill. A lower APR with a longer term, or a slightly higher rate with a shorter term, can swing thousands of dollars in either direction. This calculator amortizes **both** offers at their own amount, rate and term, then lines up the monthly payment, total interest and total cost so you can see — in black and white — which loan is actually cheaper.

Tool Features

  • Amortizes each loan at its own amount, APR and term
  • Compares monthly payment, total interest and total cost
  • Highlights the cheaper loan and the dollar difference
  • Handles a different amount or term on each side
