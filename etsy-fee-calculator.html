How the Etsy Fee Calculator Works

Every Etsy order carries three mandatory charges, plus a fourth when the sale came from one of Etsy's own ads:

Listing fee — $0.20 each time a listing is published or renewed. A listing lasts four months or until it sells, and a multi-quantity listing renews at another $0.20 each time a unit sells, so steady sellers should budget it per unit.

Transaction fee — 6.5% of everything the buyer pays, which means the item price plus shipping plus gift wrap. This is the detail that catches new sellers: charging shipping does not move that money out of the fee base.

Payment processing — 3% plus $0.25 per order for US sellers through Etsy Payments, again on the full order total.

Offsite Ads — 15% of the order total when a buyer clicks an Etsy-placed ad and buys within 30 days, or 12% once your shop has passed $10,000 in trailing-12-month sales. Above that threshold participation is mandatory for the life of the shop, and the fee is capped at $100 per order.

Put together: Your payout = order total − (listing + 6.5% transaction + 3% + $0.25 processing + Offsite Ads), and Profit = payout − your item and shipping costs.

Worked example: a $30 item with free shipping that cost you $10 to make. Transaction fee is $1.95, processing is $0.90 + $0.25 = $1.15, listing is $0.20. Total fees are $3.30, or 11.0% of the sale, leaving a payout of $26.70 and a profit of $16.70. Add shipping into the mix — a $25 item with $4.50 charged to the buyer — and the fee base becomes $29.50, so fees rise to $3.26 and the payout to $26.25 before your costs. Run that same order through a 15% Offsite Ad and fees jump to $7.68, about 26% of the order.

Mistakes That Quietly Kill Etsy Margins

Pricing from materials alone. Cost-plus-20% ignores four stacked fees and usually produces a loss once shipping labels are counted. Price from the full stack, then check the margin this calculator returns.

Treating free shipping as free. When you absorb shipping, it becomes part of your cost, and the 6.5% transaction fee still applies to the item price. Free shipping can help search ranking, but it has to be priced in, not absorbed by accident.

Ignoring Offsite Ads exposure. Shops under $10,000 a year can opt out; above that threshold the 12% rate is mandatory and permanent. A product that clears $3 of profit normally can go negative on an ad-attributed order.

Forgetting the small recurring lines. Listing renewals on multi-quantity items, currency conversion when your bank currency differs, and optional Etsy Plus or on-site ad spend all come out of the same margin.

Where This Estimate Stops

Fee schedules change and vary by country: payment processing differs outside the US, some regions add a regulatory operating fee, and Etsy adjusts rates periodically. Confirm the current schedule in your Shop Manager before setting prices from this page. The calculator also excludes on-site Etsy Ads spend, which is a marketing decision rather than a fee — fold your average ad cost per order into the cost field if you want it reflected in the margin.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Etsy take per sale in 2026?

A US seller usually loses about 10 to 13 percent of the order total to the listing fee, the 6.5 percent transaction fee and payment processing. An Offsite Ads sale adds 12 or 15 percent on top, which pushes the total past 25 percent.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5 percent transaction fee applies to shipping charged to the buyer, and processing applies to the whole order total. Charging shipping does not remove it from the fee base.

Is the $0.20 listing fee charged once or on every sale?

Once per listing, but it renews at another $0.20 each time a unit sells or after four months. On multi-quantity listings that sell steadily, budget it per unit.

What changes when my shop passes $10,000 in sales?

Offsite Ads becomes mandatory for the life of the shop and the rate falls from 15 percent to 12 percent. The fee is capped at $100 per order and applies only when a buyer clicks an Etsy-placed ad and buys within 30 days.

Why is my payout lower than my price minus 6.5 percent?

Four charges stack: the listing fee, the transaction fee, processing at 3 percent plus $0.25, and any Offsite Ads fee — all calculated on the full order total including shipping.

Does this calculator include Etsy Ads or Etsy Plus?

No. On-site ads, the optional Etsy Plus subscription and currency conversion are separate decisions rather than per-sale fees. Put your average ad cost per order into the cost field to see the impact on margin.
