Concrete Slab Calculator – Estimate Cubic Yards of Concrete For Slabs & Driveways

Pouring concrete slabs for garages, patios or driveways requires accurate volume calculation. Uneven subbase, spillage and edge trimming create unavoidable concrete waste. This free slab calculator converts length, width and slab thickness into total cubic yards with adjustable waste percentage for ready-mix concrete orders.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What thickness is required for a car driveway slab?

A: Light passenger vehicles need minimum 4 inch thickness; heavy trucks require 6+ inch reinforced concrete.

Q: How much waste should I add to concrete orders?

A: Flat simple slabs use 8–10% waste; irregular multi-shape projects need 12–15% extra concrete.
