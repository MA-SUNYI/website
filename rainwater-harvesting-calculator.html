Rainwater Harvesting Calculator – Estimate Roof Rainwater Collection Volume

Homeowners and gardeners installing rain barrels or large rain harvesting tanks often struggle to predict how much rainwater they can collect from their roof. Manual calculations require converting roof square footage, rainfall inches and accounting for typical collection loss. This free rainwater harvesting calculator estimates total gallons of runoff you can capture for irrigation, outdoor cleaning and sustainable water storage.

Tool Features

  • Standard conversion: 1 inch rain on 1 sq ft = 0.623 gallons of water
  • Adjustable loss percentage for evaporation, leaf debris and gutter overflow
  • Output raw runoff volume and usable captured rainwater in gallons
  • Helpful for sizing rain barrels, underground cistern and irrigation planning
  • Suitable for residential houses, sheds and outbuilding roof surfaces

Common Use Cases

  • Sizing rain barrel and cistern storage capacity for sustainable gardening
  • Estimating how much irrigation water you can collect during rainy seasons
  • Planning rain gutter filter systems to maximize captured rainwater
  • Comparing water savings for outdoor washing and lawn watering

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I use total roof area or footprint area?

A: Use horizontal roof footprint square footage, not sloped roof surface area for accurate results.

Q: What causes rainwater collection loss?

A: Evaporation, leaves blocking gutters, overflow, absorption on roofing material all reduce collected volume.
