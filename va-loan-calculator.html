VA Loan Calculator

The VA loan's headline perk is 0% down — but the cost that surprises buyers is the **VA funding fee**, a one-time charge that replaces the monthly mortgage insurance you'd pay on an FHA or low-down conventional loan. Enter your home price, down payment and rate to see the real VA payment (principal, interest and the funding fee rolled in) and confirm the absence of monthly MIP.
