Debt Consolidation Calculator

Consolidation only makes sense when the new loan's lower rate outweighs the extra interest from a longer term. This calculator totals your debts, estimates how long and how much interest it takes to clear them at your current minimum payments, then compares that against one consolidated loan at the rate and term you enter. You see the monthly saving **and** the lifetime interest saving side by side — the numbers that actually decide whether to refinance.

Tool Features

  • Combines up to 4 debts into one balance and weighted-average rate
  • Estimates current payoff time and interest at minimum payments
  • Compares against a single consolidation loan
  • Shows both monthly and total-interest savings
