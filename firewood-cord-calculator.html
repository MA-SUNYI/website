Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What legally defines a full cord of firewood?

A: Full cord = 128 cubic feet, standard dimensions 8ft long ×4ft high ×4ft deep of tightly stacked logs.

Q: What is the difference between a full cord and face cord?

A: A face cord only matches 8ft ×4ft front face, log depth is commonly only 16–24 inches, much less volume than full legal cord.

Q: Does loose stacking reduce actual firewood volume?

A: Yes, large gaps between logs lower usable wood volume. Measure tightly compacted stacks for most accurate comparison.
