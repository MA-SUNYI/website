Home Mortgage Down Payment

FHA Loan Calculator

An FHA loan is famous for the 3.5% down payment, but the real monthly cost hides in two kinds of mortgage insurance: a 1.75% upfront premium (UFMIP) and an annual MIP you pay every month. Enter your home price, down payment and rate to see the full FHA payment — principal, interest, both MIP layers and your total cost over the loan.
