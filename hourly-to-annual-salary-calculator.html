Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I convert hourly wage to annual salary?

A: Multiply your hourly wage by your hours per week, then by your weeks per year. For example, $25/hour × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $52,000 per year.

Q: How many hours are in a full-time work year?

A: A standard full-time schedule is 40 hours per week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours per year.

Q: Does this include overtime or bonuses?

A: No, it uses your base hourly wage and your entered hours. Add overtime or bonus income separately for a complete picture.
