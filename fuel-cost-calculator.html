How To Use This Fuel Cost Calculator

This travel expense estimator uses four simple inputs to generate full fuel consumption and cost breakdowns, usable on mobile phones while mapping out trip routes.

Step 1: Input Full Trip Mileage Distance

Enter total one-way driving miles from your mapping app for your travel route, decimals supported for partial mile segments.

Step 2: Add Vehicle MPG & Current Local Fuel Price

Fill in your car’s average miles per gallon and the current price of gasoline or diesel at local gas stations.

Step 3: Select Trip Type & Compute Fuel Expense

Choose one-way or round trip to double distance automatically, then click Calculate Fuel Cost to view total gallons needed and full estimated fuel bill.
