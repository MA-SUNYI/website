Personal Loan Calculator

A personal loan quote tells you the rate but rarely the real number you care about: what leaves your account every month, and what it costs in interest by the end. Enter the amount, rate and term below — the calculator returns your fixed monthly payment, total interest and total cost, and if you add an origination fee it shows the effective APR you actually pay.

Tool Features

  • Fixed monthly payment on a standard amortizing loan
  • Total interest and total cost over the full term
  • Effective APR after an origination fee
  • Year-by-year amortization: interest, principal and balance
  • No registration or software installation

Common Usage

  • Compare two loan offers at different rates and fees
  • See what a 3-year vs 5-year term really costs
  • Find the break-even point where extra rate beats a fee
  • Check a consolidation loan against your current payments
