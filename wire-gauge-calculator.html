Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is voltage drop important?

A: Long, thin wires create resistance. Excessive voltage drop prevents devices from operating at full power and wastes energy as heat.

Q: Does this calculator apply to aluminum wire?

A: This version uses copper conductivity values; aluminum wire requires larger gauge sizes.
