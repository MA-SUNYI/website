Fiverr Fee Calculator

Fiverr takes a flat 20 percent of every order, with no tiers, no seniority discount and no exception for tips or extras. That single number is easy to remember and easy to misprice against, because the amount you need to charge so a $500 project actually pays $500 is not $600. This calculator shows the commission, your real payout, what the buyer pays on top, and the list price that leaves you with a target amount.

How the Fiverr Fee Is Applied

The seller side is a single multiplication. Commission = order value x 20 percent. Payout = order value x 80 percent. That is the whole calculation, and it holds at $5 and at $5,000 alike. There is no volume tier to grow into.

The buyer side is separate and does not touch your payout. Fiverr adds its own service fee at checkout, roughly 5.5 percent, plus a small-order fee on orders below a set threshold. A buyer spending $100 sees about $109 at checkout on a standard US account, and the seller still receives $80. Those numbers move with region and with Fiverr's published schedule, which is why both are editable fields above.

The reverse direction is the part sellers get wrong. If you want $500 in your account, the naive answer is $500 plus 20 percent, or $600. But the commission is charged on the $600, so you keep $480 and come up $20 short. The correct formula is price = target / (1 - 0.20), which gives $625. At $625 you keep exactly $500. The gap widens with the target: a $1,000 goal needs $1,250, not $1,200.

Worked example: a $250 order with a 20 percent commission leaves $200. The buyer pays $250 + $13.75 + $0 = $263.75 once the order clears the small-order threshold. Fiverr keeps $50 of commission plus $13.75 of buyer fee, or $63.75 of the $263.75 spent, which is 24.2 percent of the buyer's money. On a $150 order the same seller keeps $120, but the buyer pays $150 + $8.25 + $3.50 = $161.75, so Fiverr's share rises to 25.8 percent because the flat fee bites harder.

Why the 20 Percent Is Not the Whole Story

Twenty percent is the visible cost. The invisible ones decide whether the work is worth taking. Withdrawal fees come off each transfer, currency conversion takes a slice if you are paid in dollars and bank in another currency, and the 14 day clearance means you are financing the platform's float on every order.

The larger cost is demand. Fiverr's commission buys access to buyers who arrive ready to pay, and that is genuinely hard to replace. The mistake is treating the commission as the only deduction and pricing a gig so that a $100 order looks like $100 of income. After commission, withdrawal, software and tax, a US seller on a small order often keeps a little over half of the headline price.

Two practical habits fix most of this. First, never price by adding 20 percent; always divide by 0.8. Second, build the fee into the listed price from the start rather than absorbing it later, because a price increase after reviews accumulate is harder to make than a slightly higher launch price. Neither changes the commission, but both change what lands in your account.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Fiverr take from a $100 order?

Exactly $20. You receive $80. The 20 percent applies to the full order value including extras, rush delivery and tips, and it does not fall as your volume rises.

Does Fiverr charge buyers on top of the gig price?

Yes. Fiverr adds a buyer service fee of roughly 5.5 percent at checkout plus a small-order fee below a threshold. That money goes to Fiverr, so the buyer's total is higher than your listed price while your payout stays at 80 percent.

What price should I list to take home a specific amount?

Divide the target by 0.8. For $500 list $625. Adding 20 percent to your target instead gives $600 and leaves you $20 short, because the commission also applies to the amount you added.

Do tips and gig extras get charged the same commission?

Yes. Tips, source-file extras and paid rush delivery all count as order value. A $50 gig with a $20 tip is a $70 order, so you keep $56 rather than $60.

When can I withdraw Fiverr earnings and what does it cost?

Earnings clear 14 days after completion, or 7 days for Top Rated sellers. Each withdrawal to PayPal, Payoneer or a bank carries a small per-transfer fee, and non-US sellers also absorb a currency conversion spread.

Is Fiverr's 20 percent cheaper than Upwork?

No, Upwork's flat freelancer service fee is currently lower. But the comparison only matters alongside order volume: a 20 percent platform that brings ten buyers can beat a cheaper one that brings two. Run both against your own order count before switching.
