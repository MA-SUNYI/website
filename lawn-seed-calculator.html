Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why do new lawns require double the seed rate of overseeding projects?

A: Bare soil needs dense seed coverage to establish full root systems; existing turf only needs sparse top-dress seed to fill bare patches.

Q: What grass type has the lowest pounds-per-1000sqft seeding requirement?

A: Warm-season bermuda and zoysia grass seeds are tiny, requiring roughly half the weight of tall fescue mixes for equal coverage.

Q: Should I add extra seed volume for shaded or sloped lawn sections?

A: Shade slows germination and slopes wash seed away easily; add a 10% extra seed buffer to calculated total weight.

Q: Are there daily limits calculating seed amounts for multiple residential lawn client projects?

A: Zero usage caps, lawn care professionals and homeowners can run unlimited grass seed calculations free all planting seasons.
