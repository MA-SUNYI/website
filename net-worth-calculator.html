Net Worth Calculator

Your net worth is everything you own minus everything you owe. Enter your total assets — cash, investments, home value, vehicles — and your total liabilities — mortgage, loans, card balances. The difference is your net worth.

Tool Features

  • Compute net worth from assets minus liabilities
  • Show the asset-to-debt ratio
  • Works for a quick annual financial snapshot
  • Handles negative net worth (debt above assets)
  • No registration or software installation

Common Usage

  • Track net worth once a year to see progress
  • Check whether debt is outpacing savings
  • Get a single number for a financial snapshot
  • Motivate a debt payoff or savings plan
