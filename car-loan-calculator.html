Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why do longer 72-month car loans cost far more total interest?

Q: How much can a larger cash down payment reduce monthly payments?

A: Every dollar of upfront cash cuts your financed principal, lowering both monthly installments and total interest accumulated over the loan term.
