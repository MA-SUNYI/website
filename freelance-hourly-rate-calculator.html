Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why not use 2080 hours for freelance annual hours?

A: 2080 is standard for full‑time employees. Freelancers spend many hours on proposals, emails and marketing which cannot be billed to clients, so real billable hours are far lower.

Q: What self‑employment tax percentage should I enter?

A: US independent contractors commonly use 15‑30% for combined income and self‑employment taxes.
