50/30/20 Monthly Budget Calculator – Split Net Income Into Needs, Wants & Savings

The 50/30/20 budgeting rule created by Elizabeth Warren remains one of the most popular simple personal finance frameworks for everyday households. Many people struggle to build consistent savings because they lack clear spending boundaries between mandatory essential bills and discretionary lifestyle purchases. The rule allocates 50% of after-tax income to Needs, 30% to Wants, and 20% to savings, investments or debt payoff. Manual percentage calculations require repeated arithmetic every time monthly income shifts. This free 50/30/20 budget calculator inputs your monthly take-home pay and instantly outputs recommended dollar limits for each category to streamline household cash flow planning.
